Classifying NMR signals with deep learning

Final-year engineering project carried out with INRAE. Goal: detect water-stress states and classify herbaceous plant species from NMR-MOUSE relaxometry signals (T2), using 1D and 2D convolutional neural networks.

Two-phase training strategy — pretraining on simulated data, then fine-tuning on real experimental data — built on a modular Python pipeline: Bayesian hyperparameter search (Optuna, TPE sampler), repeated stratified cross-validation, and explainability via Grad-CAM++.
